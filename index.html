TherapyCloud / MVP expansion + member experience

A clearer path for members.
A stronger SaaS platform.

TherapyCloud connects the public with therapists through a free directory. Therapist memberships combine a professional listing with CE Tracking, a digital-resource marketplace, blogs, events, and a peer community.

My role: Fractional Product, Growth & Delivery Lead
Scope: MVP expansion · Onboarding · Conversion · Marketplace · Content
84% fewerProfile-setup help requests after redesign
95% completeProfile completeness within 24 hours of signup
2.7% → 7.3%Checkout conversion over six months
01 / The challenge

Help members understand what to do next.

Approximately one third of help desk requests came from users asking what to do after submitting license information and purchasing a plan. The setup assumed users knew the next steps.

The broader MVP also needed stronger member tools, clearer marketplace purchasing, seller visibility, and supporting onboarding, email, and content operations.

02 / What I led

Use support patterns to redesign the journey.

I identified the need for a visible path through profile setup and redesigned the experience around five numbered sections, a step indicator, a progress bar, and clearer field guidance. Account settings were separated from setup.

I also connected product improvements with abandoned-cart recovery, onboarding and lifecycle email campaigns, social media strategy, and content creation.

03 / What changed

Less confusion. Faster profile completion.

Profile-setup help requests fell 84% following the redesign, and customer feedback was positive.

Previously, more than half of profiles were completed within 3-4 days. Afterward, profiles reached 95% completeness within 24 hours of signup. The remaining video introduction was typically completed 24-36 hours after plan purchase.

Through the broader conversion work, including abandoned-cart analysis and recovery automation, checkout conversion increased from 2.7% to 7.3% over six months.

CE Tracking / License requirements & renewal readiness

The original module could not house state requirements for different therapist license types and lacked visuals to compare completed CE with license requirements.

The module includes rules for all 50 states plus DC, required hours and topics, renewal windows, course allocation across licenses, visual progress, and opt-in renewal reminders. State requirements include a note to confirm rules with the board.

MonuCore / Concept to go-to-market

A new product.
The first paying customers.

MonuCore is a vertical SaaS platform for monument businesses, combining CRM, order management, and browser-based monument design.

My role: Product, Operations & Go-to-Market Lead
4 of the first 5Trial users converted to paid subscriptions
25 additionalEarly-access users joined
Enterprise signedCustomer operating five cemeteries and three funeral homes
01 / The challenge

Build the commercial foundation alongside the product.

A founder-led startup with no dedicated sales or operations team needed product structure, pricing, outreach, feedback, demos, and sales follow-through.

02 / What I led

Focus the audience, work the pipeline, and adapt.

I narrowed roughly 1,000 leads to 200 qualified prospects and developed pricing, campaigns, CRM stages, and trial messaging. I conducted outreach, calls, demos, onboarding, and sales follow-up.

The initial goal was 10 trial signups. Five joined the seven-day trial, and four converted to paid. I recommended a 60-day early-access beta, which attracted 25 additional users.

I developed an enterprise package for a prospect with five cemeteries and three funeral homes, defined required changes and a two-week development path with the owner, and the prospect signed.

03 / What changed

Early commercial validation and an enterprise opportunity.

The initial cohort showed customer willingness to pay in a specialized market. The revised early-access strategy expanded adoption and feedback, and the enterprise package created another route to sales.
